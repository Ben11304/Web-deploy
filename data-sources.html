Data sources

All sources are publicly available from U.S. federal agencies or peer-reviewed open-data platforms. Dataset Description Time period Resolution Source / DOI
Energy AssetsOpenStreetMap 2,827,591 energy infrastructure assets (205,783 non-tower primary): towers, generators, substations, plants, lines, fuel stations. Current (2024 extract) Point / Line / Polygon openstreetmap.org · ODbL license
EarthquakeUSGS NSHM 2023 Probabilistic PGA at 10% exceedance in 50 years (~475-yr return period), 50 states. Site class BC. Exposure threshold PGA ≥ 0.092 g (MMI ≥ VI, IEEE Std 693-2018). 2023 model · 475-yr return 0.2° contours Petersen et al. (2024) [1]; 10.5066/P9GNPCOD
FloodFEMA NFHL National Flood Hazard Layer: SFHA zone polygons (1% annual chance flood). Binary point-in-polygon classification (44 CFR § 59.1). Regulatory · 1% annual chance Polygon (parcel-level) USA Flood Hazard Areas [3] · 44 CFR § 59.1
WildfireUSGS WFPI Wildland Fire Potential Index: satellite-derived daily fire potential. P95 of daily rasters computed per pixel. Exposure threshold WFPI P95 ≥ 75. 2008–2025 (daily) 1 km USGS EROS; Burgan et al. (1998) [4]
HurricaneNOAA IBTrACS v04r01 International Best Track Archive: tropical cyclone tracks, wind speed, wind radii. Holland (1980) wind swath reconstruction. Exposure threshold ≥ 64 kt (Cat 1+). 1842–2024 (183 years) 6-hourly track points Knapp et al. (2010) [5]; NCEI IBTrACS
Winter StormNOAA SNODAS Snow Data Assimilation System: daily gridded SWE. P95 of annual maximum SWE computed per pixel. Preliminary threshold SWE P95 ≥ 100 mm (CONUS only — Alaska / Hawaii excluded). 2003–2025 (daily) 1 km (CONUS) Barrett (2003) [6]; NSIDC; 10.7265/N5TB14TC
Social VulnerabilityCDC/ATSDR SVI Social Vulnerability Index: county-level percentile ranking across 4 themes (socioeconomic, household, minority, housing). n_eligible=3,072 for per-hazard OR analysis. 2022 County-level CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022 [7]
Power OutageDOE EAGLE-I County-level power outage event counts and customer impacts at 15-min intervals. 2022 + 2024 used as response in spatial lag model (SLM). 2022, 2024 County-level Tansakul et al. (2025) [8]; 10.13139/OLCF/2500278
