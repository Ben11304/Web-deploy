02About the project

A data-driven framework for multi-hazard exposure of US energy infrastructure.

This atlas integrates 2.8 million energy assets with five hazard models, social vulnerability indices, and observed impact data — producing per-asset exposure scores and county-level risk surfaces across all fifty states.
