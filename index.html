Approach · 00

A data-driven framework, fully traceable to source

The assessment integrates federal hazard models, social vulnerability indices, and observed impact data — producing per-asset exposure classifications and county-level risk surfaces. Every threshold cites a U.S. federal agency or peer-reviewed paper; nothing is inferred, simulated, or averaged across sources. Headline numbers report the non-tower primary stratum (n=—); all-assets sensitivity (n=—) is reported in parallel.
