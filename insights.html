Insights · Statistics & assumptions · v3-PSHA-FEMA

What the data shows — and what it assumes

Headline statistics for five modeled hazards, the social-vulnerability association per hazard, and the decomposition that explains it. The central finding is heterogeneity across hazards, and that geography — not demographic indexing — accounts for most or all of the exposure gap. Every number renders from analytics-data.json (single source of truth); no value is hardcoded. Assumptions and caveats are stated in full, not hidden.

Insight · B

Geography dominates the vulnerability gap

Oaxaca–Blinder twofold decomposition (pooled reference, B=500 bootstrap) splits each hazard's high-vs-low SVI exposure gap into a part explained by geography (NERC region + census division) and an equity-attributable residual. For earthquake, flood, and wildfire, geography explains ≥100% of the gap — the equity residual is statistically indistinguishable from zero.

—
Engineering implication. Because geography explains most or all of the SVI–exposure gap for EQ / Flood / Wildfire (equity residual ≈ 0), the actionable target is hardening by NERC region and census division, not uniform SVI thresholding. Only Hurricane retains a small positive equity residual (+1.31 pp); Winter's residual is inverse.

Insight · E

Assumptions & caveats — stated in full

These conditions shape every number above. They are reported openly so readers can judge the evidence directly.

Denominator
Non-tower is primary
Headlines use the non-tower stratum (n=—). All-assets (n=—, including — towers) is reported in parallel as sensitivity, never as the headline.
Uncertainty
Bootstrap CIs are lower bounds
95% CIs come from asset-level stratified bootstrap (B=1000, stratify by state). Hazards cluster at county level, so these intervals understate true uncertainty — they are lower bounds. Spatial-cluster bootstrap is backlog (Risk #2).
Winter storm
Preliminary threshold
SWE P95 ≥ 100 mm (NOAA SNODAS) is a preliminary cutoff pending distribution review; winter results should be read as provisional.
Coverage
CONUS-only for two hazards
Wildfire (WFPI) and Winter (SNODAS) are CONUS-only rasters. Alaska and Hawaii = No Data for these two hazards, not zero exposure.
Earthquake
Probabilistic (PSHA)
USGS NSHM 2023, 475-yr return-period PGA ≥ 0.092 g (MMI ≥ VI). This is a probabilistic hazard model, not a historical event catalog.
Multiple testing
Bonferroni m=—
Significance is judged against a family of — tests (cutoff —). Earthquake's SVI association does not survive this correction; Winter's association is inverse.
